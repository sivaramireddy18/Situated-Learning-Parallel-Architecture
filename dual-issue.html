CASE STUDY 2

Dual issue, physical register renaming and precise commit

A 3-cycle multiplier and 1-cycle integer ALU show why out-of-order execution needs dependency tracking and precise state.

Instruction stream

I1: MUL R1,R2,R3 I2: ADD R4,R5,R6 I3: ADD R1,R7,R8 I4: SUB R9,R1,R10

Initial issue: I1 + I2. They use different functional resources and have no dependency between them.

Execution story

Physical register renaming

InstructionRenamed viewPurpose
I1R1 → P1Unique destination for the MUL result.
I3R1 → P2Removes the false WAW storage conflict.
I4Reads P2Uses the latest R1 mapping.

Reservation stations hold instructions until operands and functional units are ready. The reorder buffer (ROB) lets completed work become architecturally visible in program order, preserving precise state.
