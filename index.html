Industry mindset

Architecture is not isolated from the product. A pipeline bubble can consume deadline margin; a dual-issue design must manage resources and dependencies; branch prediction changes wasted cycles; and tooling helps validate whether an optimization actually matters.

Core question: What is the workload, what resource limits it, and what measurable system requirement does the optimization improve?
