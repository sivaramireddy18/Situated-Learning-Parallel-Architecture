Situated-learning reflection

Working through the examples makes the connection between processor architecture and embedded timing clearer. A single pipeline bubble may look small, but repeated inside a high-rate control loop it consumes timing margin. Branch prediction and instruction-level parallelism similarly determine how much work can fit inside a fixed real-time budget.

Example: 200 MHz × 10 µs = 2,000 cycles available per control iteration.
