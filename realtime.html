ENGINEERING CONTEXT

Turn architecture metrics into a real-time budget

The most useful industry connection is translating cycles into a deadline: compute capacity becomes a resource that must be budgeted.

Example clock

200 MHz

One clock period = 5 ns.

Control deadline

10 µs

Maximum time available per control iteration.

Cycle budget

2,000 cycles

200 MHz × 10 µs.

Budget equation

Available cycles = clock frequency × deadline
200,000,000 × 0.000010 = 2,000 cycles

The software stack must fit acquisition, computation, communication, interrupt handling and output within that budget, with appropriate safety and worst-case margin.

Where the case studies fit

  • Pipeline: hazards and memory latency affect instruction timing.
  • Dual issue: parallel execution can increase throughput when resources and dependencies permit.
  • Branch prediction: fewer mispredictions reduce wasted cycles.
  • Tooling: profiling and simulation help identify the real bottleneck before optimizing.

Engineering checklist

Measure

Profile representative workloads.

Model

Check dependencies, resources and memory behavior.

Validate

Test timing under realistic and stress conditions.
