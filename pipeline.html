Hazard & forwarding control

RAW load-use: I2 needs R1 produced by I1. The load result is not available soon enough for I2's EX stage, so the control logic freezes PC and IF/ID for one cycle and writes a bubble into ID/EX.

ALU forwarding: I3 needs R3 from I2. The result is available through the EX/MEM path and is selected by the forwarding MUX, avoiding another stall.

Embedded-system interpretation

At 200 MHz, one clock is 5 ns. The single load-use bubble adds one 5 ns cycle in this simplified model. In a high-rate control loop, repeated dependency stalls can accumulate into measurable latency and may reduce timing margin.
