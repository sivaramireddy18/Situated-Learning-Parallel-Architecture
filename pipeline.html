Hazard & forwarding unit

RAW check: I2 reads R1 produced by the preceding load I1. Because the value is not available early enough for I2's EX stage, the interlock holds IF/ID and inserts a bubble into ID/EX.

Forwarding check: I3 needs R3 from I2. The ALU result can be forwarded from the EX/MEM path into I3's EX inputs, so no additional stall is required.
