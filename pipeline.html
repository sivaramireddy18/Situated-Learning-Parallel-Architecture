Hazard and forwarding logic

C4: I2 requires R1 from the load I1. The load value is only available after MEM, so PC and IF/ID are held and ID/EX receives a bubble.

C6: I3 requires R3 from I2. The ALU result is forwarded from EX/MEM to I3's EX input, avoiding a second stall.
